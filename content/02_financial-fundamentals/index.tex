\section{Financial Fundamentals}\label{sec:financial-fundamentals}
This section provides the financial background for the remainder of the thesis. It begins with general concepts of financial markets, returns and risk, and contrasts equity with commodities as asset classes. The section then focuses on the European natural gas market and the TTF hub. The futures and option contracts traded on it are introduced next, together with implied volatility. Finally, the stylized facts of commodity returns relevant for this thesis are described.
