\section{Financial Fundamentals}\label{sec:financial-fundamentals}
This section provides the necessary background for the remainder of the thesis. It is divided into a financial and a technical part. 
The financial part covers the European natural gas market, futures contracts, options and stylized facts of commodity returns. The technical part introduces the machine learning methods used in this thesis. It begins with the terminology of neural networks, followed by recurrent architectures. The generative adversarial network (GAN) is introduced, followed its conditional, forecasting and financial variants.
